# Homiqo — Keyword Map (Pakistan)

## Data source and limits
Keywords below come from **Google autocomplete with Pakistan settings** (`gl=pk`, collected 3 Nov 2026). Autocomplete shows what people in Pakistan actually type, ordered roughly by popularity, but it gives **no search volumes or difficulty**. None are invented here. To add volumes, use Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) or Search Console → Performance once the site has impressions.

### Key insights from the data
1. **"Price in Pakistan" is the dominant transactional pattern**: "electric chopper price in pakistan", "vegetable chopper price in pakistan", "knife sharpener price in pakistan", "mandoline slicer price in pakistan", "nebulizer machine price in pakistan". Product titles use this pattern.
2. **People say "coffee beater", not "milk frother"**: "coffee beater price in pakistan", "coffee beater rechargeable". The frother is now named **"Electric Coffee Beater & Milk Frother"**.
3. **"Daraz" appears in many suggestions.** Shoppers compare with Daraz, so clear price, cash on delivery and returns information matters on every page.
4. Use-case modifiers: "handheld vacuum cleaner **for car** / **for sofa**", "sink rack **for kitchen**", "sponge holder **for kitchen sink**", "portable nebulizer **for kids**".
5. Roman Urdu terms with real demand: **"phenti hui coffee"** (beaten coffee) and **"qeema / keema machine"**. They're used only where they fit the product.

### Avoiding cannibalisation
- Category pages target the **general** term ("vegetable chopper", "electric chopper"). Product pages target the **specific product + price** term ("16-in-1 vegetable chopper price in Pakistan").
- The 16-in-1 chopper (press-down dicer) and the mandoline slicer target different terms: "vegetable chopper" vs "mandoline slicer".
- The kitchen sink organizer (counter caddy) and the faucet rack target different terms: "kitchen sink organizer rack" vs "sink rack / faucet rack".

## Keywords deliberately not used
- Competitor brand names ("philips", "kenwood", "anex", "westpoint", "daraz", "naheed", "imtiaz"). We don't sell those brands, so targeting them would mislead people.
- City-specific terms ("… karachi", "… lahore"). There are no city pages, because Homiqo delivers nationwide from one place and city landing pages would be misleading.
- "Coffee maker". That product is no longer sold.
