# Homiqo — Content Strategy

## Recommendation: a small, high-quality buying-guide section
Yes, Homiqo would benefit from a **Guides** section, but only a handful of genuinely useful articles tied to products actually in stock. With 9 products, 4–6 strong guides will do far more than dozens of thin posts. Nothing has been published automatically. Each guide should be written or checked by someone who has used the products, with real photos where possible.

### Why it helps
- It captures **informational** searches that product pages can't rank for, such as "kitchen gadgets list" and "kitchen organizer ideas".
- It builds topical authority around kitchen tools in Pakistan.
- Each guide links naturally to 2–4 product and category pages, which strengthens internal linking.
- Guides are useful assets to share on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.

Not recommended: medical or health-advice content for the nebulizer (e.g. "nebulizer medicine for kids"). It's sensitive health territory, and the only safe position is "use as prescribed by your doctor".

### Format for each guide
- URL: `/guides/<slug>`. One H1, logical H2/H3 headings, 800–1,500 words, original photos with descriptive alt text.
- `Article` structured data, a visible author/updated date, and breadcrumbs (Home › Guides › Title).
- 2–4 contextual links to products and categories with descriptive anchor text, plus a short "Shop the products" block.
- No keyword stuffing and no AI-spun filler. If a guide can't add something original, don't publish it.

## Product content (ongoing)
- **Add real specifications** to each product as soon as you have them: size, capacity, wattage, battery and charge time, materials, what's in the box. Put them in the description under a "## Specifications" heading using "- " bullet lines (the description renderer turns these into a heading and a list).
- **Add your own product videos.** Real usage videos help conversion more than stock footage.
- **Reviews:** collect genuine reviews after delivery, for example with a WhatsApp follow-up. A review feature with admin approval can be built. Never add made-up reviews.

## Social and off-site (builds authority)
- Link Instagram and Facebook posts to specific product pages, not just the home page.
- When working with influencers, ask them to link to the product page, and use a UTM tag so their traffic shows up in Admin → Analytics.
- Add the store to Google Merchant Center (free listings) using `/feed.xml`.
