### Google Merchant Center setup (free product listings)
1. Go to https://merchants.google.com, create an account and choose **Pakistan** / **PKR**.
2. Business info: add `https://www.homiqoofficial.site` and verify and claim it. Your Search Console verification lets you do this in one click.
3. **Products → Feeds → Add primary feed**: country Pakistan, language English, choose **Scheduled fetch** and enter `https://www.homiqoofficial.site/feed.xml`, fetching daily.
4. **Shipping**: add a service for Pakistan, Rs 250, free over Rs 5,000, 2–5 days.
5. **Returns**: 7 days.
6. Turn on **Free listings** under Growth → Manage programs.
7. The feed uses your **selling price only** (not the "was" price). Google requires any sale price to be a price you genuinely charged before, so the struck-through price isn't sent.

## After launch: monitoring routine
- **Weekly:** Search Console → Pages (indexing errors), Performance (queries, clicks), Enhancements (Product snippets / Merchant listings).
- **Weekly:** GA4 → Reports → Monetization → E-commerce purchases; Admin → Analytics funnel.
- **Monthly:** re-run PageSpeed Insights on mobile, and check the Merchant Center feed for disapprovals.
