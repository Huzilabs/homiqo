## 6. Campaign setup (suggested starting point)
- **Objective:** Sales → Website → Pixel 1461422069206467 → **Purchase**
- **Location:** Pakistan. Start with the major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan) if the budget is small.
- **Audience:** Advantage+ audience, or broad 20–45 with interests such as coffee, Nescafé, baking, cooking, home & kitchen and online shopping. Treat these as starting hypotheses, not proven audiences.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements (Reels and Stories usually suit product demos)
- **Budget:** start around **Rs 1,500–2,000/day** per ad set with **3–4 creatives**. Don't judge or edit for the first **3 days**, so Meta's learning phase can run.

## 7. Know your numbers (decide the cut-off before launching)
- Selling price Rs 1,399 − product cost Rs 550 = **Rs 849 gross margin per unit**, before PostEx charges, returns and packaging.
- **Break-even cost per purchase = Rs 849 − (PostEx charge + packaging + an allowance for refused COD parcels).** Fill in your real PostEx rate.
- If cost per purchase stays above break-even after 3–4 days, change the creative or the audience; don't just raise the budget.
- Confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Refused COD parcels are the biggest hidden cost.
